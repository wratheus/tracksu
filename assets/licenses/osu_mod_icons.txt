The 72 PNG files bundled in assets/icon_mods are byte-identical to the
corresponding files at the source revision above: 70 glyphs from Icons/Mods
(the "mod-" prefix is dropped from the file name) and the mod-icon and
mod-icon-extender shapes from Icons/BeatmapDetails. Tracksu only tints them
at runtime; the image files are not modified.
